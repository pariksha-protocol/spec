# 11. Security Considerations

S1 — The chain authorises and witnesses; the enclave decrypts. No decryption key or share ever touches the ledger; reconstruction occurs only in secure enclaves and is zeroised on use. S2 — Audited primitives only; no novel cryptography anywhere in a conforming Implementation. S3 — Separation of masters (§3.1 R4) is structural, not procedural: a quorum whose proofs chain to one root MUST fail verification. S4 — Bounded variance: per-receiver policy freedom is drawn from enumerated sets, and each variance references a witnessed justification; a compromised issuer cannot quietly weaken one context's rules. S5 — Privacy: the ledger is not a directory (§8 E5); v1.1 zero-knowledge presentations remove identity from the ledger entirely. S6 — Honest residue: within the plaintext window, capture by a present, credentialed insider is not preventable — it is compressed (minutes), attributed (watermark + named custody), and contained (per-context artifacts at later rungs). The Protocol's guarantee is that theft outside the witnessed, quorum-held window is cryptographically meaningless, and inside it is named, timed, and localised.
