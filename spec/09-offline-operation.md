# 9. Offline Operation

Offline is a first-class mode, not degradation. The invariant: everything that requires the network happens before exam day; exam day verifies signatures and matches biometrics locally. During the last connected sync a context receives its sealed Paper Credentials, its roster of Admit/Candidate Credentials with sealed biometric templates, and Capability Tokens pre-authorising the window's grants. Offline, the validator verifies and grants locally; all events chain into the sealed local log. On reconnection the buffered subtree checkpoints (§6.3); an unbroken chain proves nothing was inserted, altered, or reordered while dark. Submission and transmission are distinct: a Response Credential is complete when its chain closes on-device, regardless of when it uploads. Cutting a context's connectivity therefore gains an attacker nothing and costs the examination nothing.
